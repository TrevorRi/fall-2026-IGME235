Portal: 2007

Description:

Portal is a 3D action-puzzle platformer in which you take the role of a test subject named "Chell" who awakens to a blank white laboratory space and is directed by a wise-cracking AI named ""
to travel through several locally contained portals in order to traverse seemingly impossible paths. After a short period of directing Chell around, GlaDOS grants Chell the ability to create her own
portals using an aptly named "Portal Gun". Chell is then directed to continue, using her new gun to solve puzzles, avoid obstacles, and destroy enemies.

Categories:

  • Genre: Action
  • Perspective: First Person
  • Players: One player only
  • Gameplay: Puzzle/ Platforming
